\noindent\textbf{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item First WTP estimates for battery-health attributes in the U.S. used BEV market
  \item Range valued at \$11,070 per 100 mi; degradation penalized \$980 per pct-pt/yr
  \item Pack- and cell-level battery refurbishment each reduce WTP by over \$4,000
  \item Six consumer segments differ in battery-health sensitivity and buyer characteristics
  \item Battery cost and warranty disclosure does not reduce market opt-out rates
\end{itemize}
